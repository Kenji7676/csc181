## 1. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  
I am designing for local residents or visitors who are going to Mugna.

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  
Mugna was relocated from the KCC Grounds of Brgy. Saray-Tibanga to a more remote location at the Ludo Grounds in Brgy. Santiago which might be less familiar than what people were already accustomed to. There are also a lot of stalls so finding the exact location of desired stalls or products is a daunting task.

---

## 2. Situation or need

**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**  
These people want to go to Mugna to explore the stalls, purchase goods sold there, and maybe even go for carnival rides or watch concerts.

---

## 3. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**  
Considering the relocation, people might be confused as to how to get to the new Mugna location. Also, based on personal experience, I was trying to find Dubai Chewy Cookie when I last visited, and I spent a huge amount of time roaming the entire Mugna grounds only to find out that no stalls sold the product.

---

## 4. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?**  
I propose an application that serves as a map for the entire Mugna grounds which details the locations of the entrace/exit gates, what stalls are present and where they're stationed, and what products are sold in each stall. This app also includes guides on how to get to the new Mugna location from wherever they are in Iligan.

**How would it help the intended users?**  
This helps people, especially those unfamiliar with the new location or those trying to find specific stalls or products, to easily locate where they need to go for whatever purpose they have in Mugna. This saves a lot of time and effort on their end.

---

## 5. Things users can do

Describe **two specific actions** that users could perform using your proposed system.

1. They can input where they are in Iligan and the app shows a step-by-step guide on how to get to Mugna.
2. They can view or search for what stalls or products are present and where it is located.

## 6. Important qualities

Identify **two qualities** that would make your proposed system useful. You may consider whether it should be easy to use, fast, reliable, safe, private, accessible, multilingual, low-data, clear, or available during high demand.

### Quality 1: Available during High Demand

**Why does this matter to users?**  
Since Mugna is often crowded, if this application does exist, a lot of people might try to use it to make navigating around Mugna a lot easier. Hence, it must be able to handle high traffic and demand without breaking down or getting disrupted.

### Quality 2: Real-Time Updates

**Why does this matter to users?**  
There might be changes with the layout of the Mugna interior, so the app must be able to update as soon as possible so that users are given up-to-date information.

## 7. How to tell whether the solution helped

How could you determine whether your proposed solution actually helped users?

I would ask users for feedback which would help me track how fast users are able to find their desired stalls and products, and how easy it is to navigate around Mugna.
